Why Do You Need a Professional Website?

Some business owners rely on social media pages alone, but the reality is that a website offers advantages no other platform can match.

Having a professional website helps you:

  • Build trust with customers
  • Appear in Google search results
  • Present your services and products professionally
  • Receive customer requests 24 hours a day
  • Increase your chances of making sales
  • Reduce your reliance on paid ads alone
  • Strengthen your brand identity
  • Collect and analyze customer data to improve performance

A website is not just a set of pages with information about your business. It is a marketing tool that works continuously to help you reach new customers and grow your business.

Our Web Design Project Process

At Brandiva, we follow a clear work plan to deliver your project to the highest quality and on time.

  1. 01

    Understanding your business

    We start by learning about the nature of your business, the services you offer, your target audience, and the goals of the website, so we can build a site that serves your real needs.

  2. 02

    Competitor research

    We analyze competing websites in your field to identify their strengths and the opportunities available, then work to deliver a better experience that helps you stand out.

  3. 03

    Planning the website structure

    Before design begins, we set out a clear website structure covering the main pages, sections, and navigation paths, to ensure ease of use and improve the visitor experience.

  4. 04

    Designing user interfaces

    We design professional interfaces that combine beauty and simplicity, with a focus on highlighting your brand identity and encouraging visitors to take the desired action.

  5. 05

    Website development

    Once the design is approved, it is turned into a fast, responsive website using the latest technologies, with performance and security in mind.

  6. 06

    Website testing

    Before launch, we run comprehensive tests to verify:

    • Loading speed
    • Compatibility of all pages
    • Forms working correctly
    • Compatibility with different browsers
    • Compatibility with mobile phones
  7. 07

    Website launch

    Once the website is confirmed ready, it is published on hosting, connected to the domain, and set up to run efficiently.

Web design FAQ

How much does a website cost?

The cost of a website depends on several factors, such as the number of pages, the type of website, the required functionality, and whether it includes an online store, a booking system, or multiple languages. That is why we provide custom quotes that fit the needs of each project.

How long does it take to design a website?

The timeline varies with the size of the project, but informational websites usually take one to three weeks, while online stores or larger projects may need longer depending on the requirements.

Will the website be mobile-friendly?

Yes, every website we design is fully responsive across smartphones, tablets, and computers to ensure the best user experience.

Can I edit the website content after delivery?

Absolutely. The website is delivered with an easy-to-use control panel that lets you edit text and images and add pages or articles without any coding experience.

Does web design include search engine optimization (SEO)?

We design websites according to SEO best practices in terms of site speed, page structure, headings, and links, so the website is ready for you to start improving its search rankings. We also offer a full SEO service for those who want to increase their visibility on Google.

Can you design an online store?

Yes, we offer professional online store design with easy product management, payment method integration, shipping company integration, and simple order management.

Can the website be linked to social media?

Yes, the website can be linked to your Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and other accounts, making it easier to communicate with customers and strengthening your digital presence.

Does the service include buying the domain and hosting?

We can help you choose the right domain and provide fast, secure hosting, as well as fully set up the website so it is ready to go.

Can the website be expanded in the future?

Yes, websites are built in a way that allows you to add new pages, services, or functionality at any time without having to redesign the entire website.

Will the website be fast?

We optimize the website's loading speed using best practices such as compressing images, improving code, and choosing strong hosting, which helps improve the user experience and the site's search ranking.

What is the difference between a website and social media pages?

Social media pages are important for engaging with customers, but they are no substitute for a website. A website gives your business a professional identity, lets you appear in search results, gives you full control over your content, and builds greater customer trust.

Do you offer technical support after the website is delivered?

Yes, we provide technical support to help you solve problems and answer your questions, as well as periodic maintenance and update services.

Can more than one language be added to the website?

Yes, multilingual websites can be designed to suit companies that target customers in different countries and markets.

Is the website secure against hacking?

We are committed to applying security best practices, such as using SSL certificates, system updates, and protection settings, which helps reduce risks and keep the website's data safe.

Can my current website be moved to new hosting?

Yes, we offer a website migration service to new hosting that preserves your data and keeps downtime to a minimum.

What information do I need to provide before the project starts?

We usually need your company logo, an overview of your business, your services or products, your contact details, and any images or content you want to include, and we will help you complete anything that is missing.

Do you offer custom designs or rely on ready-made templates?

We rely on custom designs that suit each business's identity, with the option of using flexible solutions when needed, striking a balance between quality and budget.

Why should I choose Brandiva to design my website?

Because we focus on delivering websites that combine professional design, fast performance, ease of use, and search engine compatibility, with attention to achieving your business goals, not just building a website.
