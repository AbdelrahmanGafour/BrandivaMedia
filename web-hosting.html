Why Is Website Hosting Important?

You may have a professional design and outstanding content, but if your hosting is slow or unstable, it will hurt the user experience and your search engine ranking.

Choosing the right hosting helps you:

  • Improve your website's loading speed
  • Reduce the chance of downtime
  • Protect website and customer data
  • Improve the user experience
  • Increase your chances of appearing in Google results
  • Handle a larger number of visitors
  • Scale easily as your business grows

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I need hosting if I already have a website?

Yes, a website can't run online without hosting that provides the space and operating resources it needs.

What's the difference between a domain and hosting?

A domain is your website's address on the internet, while hosting is where your website files and databases are stored.

What is the best type of hosting for a WordPress website?

It depends on your website's size and number of visitors, but WordPress hosting or VPS is usually a suitable choice for most projects.

Can I move my website to Brandiva hosting?

Yes, we move the entire website while preserving your data and minimizing downtime.

Do you provide professional email?

Yes, we can provide email linked to your company's domain name.

Do you offer website backups?

Yes, we provide backup solutions that help restore your website when needed.

Is an SSL certificate necessary?

Yes, it protects user data and increases visitor trust, and it is also one of the factors that affect search engine optimization.

Can I upgrade my hosting plan later?

Absolutely, the plan can be upgraded easily as your website grows and your visitor numbers increase.
