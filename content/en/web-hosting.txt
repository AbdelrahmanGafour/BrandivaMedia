Yes, a website can't run online without hosting that provides the space and operating resources it needs.
What's the difference between a domain and hosting?
A domain is your website's address on the internet, while hosting is where your website files and databases are stored.
What is the best type of hosting for a WordPress website?
It depends on your website's size and number of visitors, but WordPress hosting or VPS is usually a suitable choice for most projects.
